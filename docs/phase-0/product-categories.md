# Product Categories

For the MVP, we focus on two packaged commodity categories with clearly defined declaration requirements.

## 1. Packaged Food (e.g., Biscuits, Snacks)
- **Applicable declarations:** Product name, Manufacturer/Packer address, Net weight/volume, MRP, Date of packing/manufacturing, Expiry/Best Before date, Consumer care details, FSSAI logo/license number, Veg/Non-veg symbol.
- **Applicable rules:** Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011; FSSAI labeling guidelines (as applicable to LM).
- **Special conditions/exemptions:** Wholesale packages or packages below 10g/10ml may have exemptions for certain declarations.
- **Visual requirements:** Principal display panel rules apply; mandatory declarations must be grouped together. Veg/Non-veg symbol must be clearly visible and of specified dimensions.
- **Checks supported by the MVP:** MRP presence and format, Date format, Net quantity presence, Consumer care presence, Manufacturer details presence.
- **Checks requiring manual verification:** Verification of precise physical font size requirements based on principal display panel area.

## 2. Cosmetics (e.g., Shampoo, Soap)
- **Applicable declarations:** Product name, Manufacturer name and address, Net quantity, MRP, Date of manufacture, Use before date, Consumer care details, Batch number.
- **Applicable rules:** Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011; Drugs and Cosmetics Rules.
- **Special conditions/exemptions:** Packages less than 10g or 10ml are exempt from declaration of MRP and dates.
- **Visual requirements:** Clear legibility, appropriate contrast.
- **Checks supported by the MVP:** Extraction of MRP, quantity, dates, manufacturer details, and consumer care. Check for missing elements.
- **Checks requiring manual verification:** Ambiguous exemptions (e.g., promotional free samples).
