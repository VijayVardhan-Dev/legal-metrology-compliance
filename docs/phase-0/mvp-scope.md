# MVP Scope

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) focuses on a limited, demonstrable set of compliance checks rather than attempting to implement every Legal Metrology provision.

## Supported Declaration Categories
The MVP will support extraction and validation of the following categories:
1. Product identification/name
2. Manufacturer / packer / importer details
3. Required address details
4. Net quantity
5. MRP
6. Date-related declaration (Manufacturing, Packing, or Import date)
7. Consumer-care/contact details
8. Specific mandatory declarations applicable to chosen product categories (e.g., "Best before" for food).

## Supported Visual Checks
The MVP will include the following visual assessments:
9. Declaration readability (based on image clarity/OCR confidence)
10. Approximate text/font-size assessment (where technically measurable relative to package dimensions or standard references)
11. Declaration visibility (detecting obscured or cut-off text)
12. Missing declaration detection (flagging required categories that were not found)

## Out of Scope Items for MVP
- Full coverage of every Legal Metrology rule and exemption.
- Automatic legal prosecution/enforcement or issuance of penalties.
- Perfect physical font-size measurement from arbitrary photographs.
- Automatic interpretation of ambiguous legal cases.
- Guaranteed 100% OCR accuracy.
- Automatic final legal decision without officer verification.
